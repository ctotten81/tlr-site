Need help?

The Locker Room is a sports-first social platform for high school student-athletes, with NIL disclosure and compliance built in.

For any question, problem, or request — including account help, content reports, and parent or guardian requests — email us:

casey@tlrsports.io

We aim to respond within two business days.

For parents and guardians

If your student-athlete uses The Locker Room, you can:

  • Review your child’s account and the information on it
  • Approve or decline any NIL deal before it is disclosed to their school
  • Request corrections to their information
  • Request deletion of their account and information

Email us at casey@tlrsports.io and we will help you directly.

Reporting content

Every post and profile in the app has a ⋯ menu with options to report or block. Reported content is reviewed and acted on. If you need to report something urgently, email us and we will respond as quickly as we can.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time from inside the app: tap your profile icon, then Delete Account. This removes your profile, posts, and account data.

Note: if you have disclosed an NIL deal, a record of that disclosure is retained for your school’s compliance and PIAA reporting obligations, as described in our Privacy Policy.
