NIL Compliance

The part nobody else does

Pennsylvania gives an athlete a short window to disclose an NIL deal to their athletic director. Most schools run that on a paper form with two signatures. The Locker Room runs it properly.

  • Guardian approval is enforced, not assumed. A minor's deal cannot be accepted until a linked parent or guardian approves it.
  • The disclosure clock is tracked. Each deal carries the date it was signed and the date it was disclosed.
  • Athletic directors get a record they can export. A dated, signed PDF for the PIAA, not a spreadsheet someone keeps by hand.
  • College reporting starts in high school. The NCAA counts deals from junior year. Athletes can export their own history for college compliance staff.
  • We never touch deal money. No cut, no payments through the platform, ever.

For athletic directors

You stay out of the middle

The school is never a party to an NIL agreement. You get visibility and a record you can produce on demand — not a role in the deal.
